MF Language Academy started as an evening English circle and grew into a full language school for adults who need results, not endless textbooks.

Who we are

Small classes. Clear progress.

Students join MF Language Academy to pass an exam, join a new team, or feel at home in another country. We keep groups small, publish the weekly plan, and measure progress with speaking checks instead of surprise tests.

Campus classes meet at Language Plaza. Online classes use the same teachers and the same materials, so a student can switch format when travel or work changes.
