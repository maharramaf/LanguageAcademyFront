Course details

English Beginner

Build a confident foundation in everyday English, from greetings and travel to simple conversations at work and in the city.

Overview

This course is for new learners who want clear structure, plenty of speaking time, and feedback they can use the same day. Classes stay small so every student is heard.

What you will practice

  • Introduce yourself and talk about daily routines
  • Use present and past forms in simple stories
  • Handle cafés, directions, and travel situations
  • Build a personal vocabulary notebook

Curriculum

Pronunciation basics, greetings, and questions you will actually use.

Home, work, food, and telling the time with natural phrases.

Directions, shopping, and polite requests.

Past events, short presentations, and a final speaking check.
